# Premium Pass Goals

## Main agent goal

Make the fresh Graft launch video feel premium without sanding off the neobrutalist hand-drawn energy. Preserve product truth: local macOS Markdown, plain files, external agents can edit the same files, and Graft makes those changes reviewable before they land.

Design dials:

- `DESIGN_VARIANCE: 8`
- `MOTION_INTENSITY: 8`
- `VISUAL_DENSITY: 5`

Success criteria:

- The video still feels like a handmade paper riot, not a generic SaaS ad.
- The palette, typography, spacing, and final frame feel intentional and high-craft.
- Major text is readable at hero-frame moments.
- HyperFrames lint, validate, and inspect have no errors.
- Final MP4 is 1920x1080, 24 seconds, with full-length audio.

## Subagent goals

### Visual craft subagent

Audit the current video through the design-taste-frontend lens. Return concrete changes that improve premium visual craft while preserving neobrutalist paper, marker, torn-edge, and zine energy.

### Motion and audio subagent

Audit GSAP timing, scene handoffs, beat use, SFX density, rendered metadata, and frame risk. Return concrete changes that improve pacing, readability, and audio polish.

### Product story subagent

Audit the video against Graft source truth. Return tighter copy and narrative recommendations that make the launch specific to Graft and avoid vague or false claims.
