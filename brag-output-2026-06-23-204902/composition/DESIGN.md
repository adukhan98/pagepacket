# Graft Paper Riot

## Style Prompt

Neobrutalist handmade launch video for a local Markdown editor. The frame should feel like a chaotic product zine taped to a wall: torn cream paper, black marker typography, hot-pink agent patches, acid-green checkpoint tape, rough ink borders, handwritten annotations, stamps, arrows, scribbles, and intentionally imperfect collage movement. It should feel aggressive, clever, and alive, not clean or corporate.

## Colors

- Paper: `#fff2c7`
- Ink: `#0a0a0a`
- Hot Pink: `#ff4d8b`
- Acid Green: `#9cff3d`
- Deep Teal: `#1a3a3a`
- Warning Yellow: `#ffd400`
Role lock: hot pink marks external agent intrusion, acid green marks human approval/checkpoints, warning yellow marks filesystem risk, and deep teal is reserved for small editorial notes.

## Typography

- Display: `Bangers`, fallback `Impact`
- Handwritten: `Permanent Marker`, fallback `Comic Sans MS`
- Mono/source: `Space Mono`, fallback `ui-monospace`

## Motion

- Hard paper slams.
- Marker wipes and rough scribble reveals.
- Jitter that feels hand-animated, but finite and deterministic.
- Stamp impacts on beat.
- No calm crossfades unless they are buried inside a rough transition.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not reuse Source Bloom assets, old scene plates, or the previous launch-video structure.
- Do not make it polished, minimal, glassy, or app-store clean.
- Do not use generic AI sparkle graphics.
- Do not invent cloud/friends/account features.
- Do not let the chaos make required text unreadable.
