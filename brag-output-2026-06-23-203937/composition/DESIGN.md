# Graft Source Bloom Launch Film

## Creative Direction

This is not the landing page in motion. It is a short product film about a document becoming a shared working surface for a person and their agents.

The video should feel like source material under a macro lens: paper fiber, ink, Markdown marks, revision strata, and luminous agent traces moving through the same local file. The landing page only informs the brand DNA: warm paper, black editorial type, graft green, and a precise local-first attitude.

## Core Message

The Markdown editor for you and your agents.

Coming soon for macOS.

## Visual System

- Light mode only.
- Full-frame asset compositions, not boxed UI sections.
- No fake app screenshots.
- No repeated card grids.
- No arrows as the main storytelling device.
- No duplicate logo plus wordmark in the final scene.
- Use generated or constructed assets as the scene material.

## Asset Pack

The composition uses local scene assets:

- `assets/source-bloom-field.svg`: macro paper field with ghost Markdown syntax and a green graft seam.
- `assets/source-bloom-folds.svg`: folded document terrain and ink shadows.
- `assets/source-bloom-agent-light.svg`: luminous agent paths passing through source material.
- `assets/source-bloom-history.svg`: revision rings and archival strata.

These are treated as full-screen editorial plates. HyperFrames supplies camera movement, exposure wipes, ink masks, and type choreography.

## Palette

- Paper: `#F4EFDF`
- Warm light: `#FFFBEF`
- Paper shadow: `#E7DEC9`
- Ink: `#08100A`
- Body ink: `#344039`
- Muted ink: `#5B625C`
- Graft green: `#048C3D`
- Pale green: `#CDEFD6`
- Highlight mint: `#DFF7E6`
- Soft peach: `#F1CDB9`

The result should be warm and light, with enough tonal depth to feel cinematic.

## Typography

- Display: `Bricolage Grotesque`
- Text: `IBM Plex Sans`
- Source marks: `IBM Plex Mono`

Use smaller, more readable text than the rejected version. Let the camera and assets carry scale.

## Story

1. A plain Markdown source appears as physical material.
2. Agent traces enter the same source instead of moving work elsewhere.
3. Human and agent edits braid together as a single local workflow.
4. History blooms as visible revision strata.
5. The final Graft lockup lands with the promise and coming soon message.

## Copy

## Motion

- Slow macro pans across oversized asset plates.
- Rough write-on paths and masks.
- Exposure wipes between scenes.
- Type appears with small editorial gestures, not huge kinetic shouting.
- No infinite loops.
- All movement must be finite and registered in the HyperFrames timeline.
