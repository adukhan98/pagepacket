# Graft Launch Video Design

## Style Prompt

Warm document calm gets hit by agent-edit chaos. The system borrows the supplied Notion-like discipline: paper-soft canvas, near-black type, one structural blue, subtle hairlines, and sticker colors as personality only. The launch film pushes that base into a premium hand-drawn neobrutalist language with torn paper, marker annotations, oversized type, and tactile review packets.

## Colors

- Paper canvas: `#f6f5f4`
- Ink: `#111111`
- Muted text: `#615d59`
- Hairline: `#e6e6e6`
- Structural blue: `#0075de`
- Pressed blue: `#005bab`
- Dark island: `#213183`
- Sticker pink: `#ff64c8`
- Sticker purple: `#d6b6f6`
- Sticker orange: `#dd5b00`
- Sticker teal: `#2a9d99`
- Sticker green: `#1aae39`
- Sticker sky: `#62aef0`

## Typography

- Display: heavy system sans, tight and direct.
- Body: system sans, clean and document-like.
- Mono: self-hosted SF Mono asset for file paths and diff lines.
- Handwritten: self-hosted Bradley Hand Bold asset for marker notes.

## Shape And Material

- Paper/cards use 12px radius.
- Controls use full pill radius.
- Stickers can be sharp, torn, or slightly rotated.
- Elevation is hairline plus subtle layered shadow, never heavy black drops.

## Motion

- Motion is motivated by state: edits arrive, Graft captures, human reviews, checkpoint lands.
- Transitions use torn paper, sticker swarms, and blue capture wipes.
- Text enters fast and then holds.
- No infinite loops.

## What Not To Do

- Do not reuse the old launch-video structure.
- Do not use generic SaaS language.
- Do not claim cloud sync, multiplayer, shared cursors, or in-app AI writing.
- Do not use purple/blue AI glow as the main aesthetic.
- Do not use em dash characters.
- Do not turn the video into a fake terminal or fake dashboard.
