# Graft Launch Video Goals

## Main agent goal

Create a fresh Graft launch video using `$brag` and `$design-taste-frontend`, following the supplied Notion-inspired design brief as a visual contract while preserving the user's requested premium neobrutalist hand-drawn and handwritten energy.

## Design read

Reading this as: a launch film for technical writers and agent-heavy Codex users, with a Notion-like document-system base pushed into a premium hand-drawn brutal motion language, leaning toward native HyperFrames, GSAP, warm paper, one structural blue accent, and decorative sticker chaos.

## Dials

- `DESIGN_VARIANCE: 9`
- `MOTION_INTENSITY: 9`
- `VISUAL_DENSITY: 5`

## Product truth lock

- Graft is a local macOS Markdown editor.
- Plain Markdown files remain canonical.
- External agents edit the same files on disk.
- Graft turns external edits into reviewable diffs, hunks, conflicts, and checkpoints.
- Avoid multiplayer, cloud sync, shared cursors, team workspace, and in-app AI writing claims.

## Subagent goals

- Story agent: audit the product claim, video copy, overclaim risks, and share caption.
- Visual agent: translate the Notion-like design brief into a premium hand-drawn neobrutalist frame system.
- Motion/audio agent: define pacing, motivated motion, audio posture, HyperFrames risks, and verification commands.

## Success criteria

- Fresh output directory, no reuse of old video structure.
- `brag-plan.md`, `composition-brief.md`, `share-copy.txt`, and `composition/` exist.
- At least one real Graft asset or product surface appears in the render.
- Video is 15-25 seconds, landscape 1920x1080, with music and SFX.
- HyperFrames `npm run check` passes or any remaining warning is explicitly explained.
- Final MP4 is rendered to `brag.mp4`.
