# Brag Plan: Graft

## What is this app?

Graft is a local macOS Markdown editor where external agents can edit the same plain files, but every agent change becomes a reviewable diff, conflict, or checkpoint before it lands.

## The angle

A calm document desk gets attacked by agent edits. Graft does not panic or hide the mess. It turns the mess into a review packet: local files, visible hunks, explicit accept/reject, and a checkpoint before anything replaces the draft.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

The line "your agent changed your markdown" appears on a warm paper canvas while hot pink handwritten edits slam across it. The calm document surface is instantly disrupted.

## Key moments (the middle)

- Plain Markdown files sit on the desk as the canonical source.
- External edits tear in as sticker-like patches and noisy handwritten annotations.
- Graft catches them as a review packet with hunks.
- The human accepts one hunk, rejects another, and keeps a checkpoint before changes land.

## Outro / punchline

Agents can edit. You decide.

## User flow worth showing

Open a local Markdown folder, let an external agent edit a file, then review proposed hunks before accepting or rejecting them.

## Audio direction

- Role: clean cinematic support with tactile UI hits.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: start at 0, medium-low bed, fade out under final logo.
- Music cue guidance: preset cues available. Strong hits near 8.74s, 13.11s, 17.47s, and 22.93s. Use them only when readability survives.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle. Paper grain and blue control surfaces may breathe with the music. No waveform visuals.
- SFX posture: moderate, low-risk, motion-matched.
- Audio-coupled moments: hook slam, agent edit cards, review packet reveal, accept/reject clicks, final logo hit.
- Restraint rule: do not make the handmade style sound cheap or comedic.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 - Agent touched the draft - 3.2s

Warm paper fills the frame. "your agent changed your markdown" appears as tight black type. Pink handwritten marks and sticker tabs hit the page.

Sequential/interaction: handwritten edits strike in one by one.

Audio intent: instant hook, one soft impact and light UI ticks.

Transition mood: torn-paper wipe to Scene 2.

### Scene 2 - Plain files are the source - 4.0s

The Graft icon and local file cards appear on a quiet desk. Copy: "plain markdown stays canonical" and "no cloud handoff."

Sequential/interaction: file cards stack in a stagger.

Audio intent: calmer, premium desk feel.

Transition mood: sticker swarm to Scene 3.

### Scene 3 - Agents write on disk - 4.4s

An agent edit bursts in from the side as a hot pink paper strip. Diffs appear: added launch plan, removed vague promise, changed risk note.

Sequential/interaction: three edit strips arrive on beat-grid timing.

Audio intent: tactile card slide and short glitch accent.

Transition mood: blue capture wipe to Scene 4.

### Scene 4 - Graft catches the edit - 5.0s

A review packet lands in the center with hunk cards and real product language: "External edit detected", "2 hunks", "conflict safe", "checkpoint ready."

Sequential/interaction: packet opens, hunk cards fan out.

Audio intent: strong reveal near 13.11s, then soft card placements.

Transition mood: accept/reject buttons snap to Scene 5.

### Scene 5 - Human decides - 4.3s

Blue accept control and ink reject control appear. One hunk is accepted, another is rejected. A checkpoint stamp lands.

Sequential/interaction: click accept, click reject, checkpoint stamp.

Audio intent: two dry clicks, one final soft hit.

Transition mood: dark indigo island to Scene 6.

### Scene 6 - Launch mark - 3.1s

Deep indigo night band with the Graft icon, the line "agents can edit. you decide.", and a handwritten tag: "plain files. reviewable agent edits."

Sequential/interaction: logo lands, line resolves, tag scribbles on.

Audio intent: final bell plus music fade.

**Music mood for this video:** cinematic and clean.

**Audio summary:** a polished music bed supports tactile paper/UI hits without turning the film into a joke.
